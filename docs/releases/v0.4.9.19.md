Preview can be launched without `resume` or modifying recovery history.

No ROM image, native binaries, guest mutable-state dumps or local paths are bundled; sampled instruction telemetry may include individual opcode bytes. Reuse of a source-matched owner-generated native translation archive is recorded explicitly and does not constitute an independent binary rebuild. GUI can be unavailable even when headless tests pass.
- Both old and recent versioned jobs remain discoverable; heavy generated output remains out of AppData. Native front-end implementation, original recovery job, plugin DLL ABI, Ghidra/Rizin analysis and 642 frozen original files remain untouched.
